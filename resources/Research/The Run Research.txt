** AOB of Vehicle Parts Memory Region: * * * * * * * * * * * * 0D 00 00 00 * * * * * * * * * * * * 60 55 84 02 * * * * * * * * * * * * 04 00 00 00 * * * * * * * * * * * * 60 55 84 02

Above each result there are 3 float values. Put all of them to 1.2 in order to enable the manual openness control. Above the 3 floats row there’s the actual openness value of each part
